      Viewing and playback facts

      Watched
      Completed viewings in the shared Ryot account. Zero means no recorded completions, not proof nobody has watched it.
      Ready to watch
      A matching movie has a media source in Jellyfin, regardless of its quality or upgrade monitoring.
      Monitored / Unmonitored
      Current Radarr acquisition policy, separate from whether a title is wanted. Monitored does not prove an upgrade is downloading or that current quality is final. Omitted when monitoring is not confirmed.
      Jellyfin availability
      Ready to watch means a playable copy is confirmed. Not available means a successful check found no playable copy. Check unavailable means readiness could not be confirmed; see Library check for the reason. Jellyfin does not acquire movies; Radarr handles acquisition and monitoring.
      Audio
      Languages from audio tracks of current playback copies, not subtitles, desired profiles or release advertisements.
